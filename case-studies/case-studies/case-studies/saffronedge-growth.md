# SaffronEdge — B2B Growth & GTM

## Context

I led the D2C & Retail Technology business at SaffronEdge, owning positioning, GTM, demand generation and P&L.

## Growth Challenge

The business needed a repeatable growth engine rather than relying primarily on individual sales opportunities.

## What I Built

I worked across:

- Positioning & messaging
- SEO & organic growth
- Content
- Outbound
- Performance marketing
- ABM
- CRM
- Lead scoring
- Marketing automation
- Lifecycle programs
- Sales enablement

I also led a 12-person core team and worked with a broader 28-member operations organization.

## Results

### Revenue
**$200K → $2M annual revenue**

### MRR
**$20K → $38K+**

### ABM
**45+ high-intent MQLs**

### Client Acquisition
**14+ fintech / retail technology clients onboarded in 8 months**

## Key Takeaway

The biggest growth gains came from connecting positioning, acquisition, qualification and lifecycle — rather than treating each marketing channel independently.
