# Videomagic — Activation & Onboarding

## Context

Videomagic is an AI video platform where product adoption depended heavily on how quickly new users could understand the product and reach their first meaningful outcome.

## Problem

The onboarding experience contained friction that affected activation and repeat usage.

The objective was to reduce time-to-value and help users reach the product's core value faster.

## Approach

I worked across product, marketing and growth to:

- Identify onboarding friction
- Review the activation journey
- Simplify the path to first value
- Improve activation messaging and education
- Strengthen lifecycle communication
- Monitor activation and repeat usage

## Outcome

The changes resulted in:

- **40%+ improvement in activation and repeat usage**
- **~35% reduction in time-to-value**

The broader growth program helped Videomagic reach:

- **7,500+ users in under 120 days**
- **$50K+ MRR within 6 months**

## What I Learned

Activation isn't just an onboarding problem.

It is a product + messaging + lifecycle problem.

The strongest growth opportunities often come from removing friction between acquisition and the user's first successful outcome.
