# AI Feature Launch — Product GTM

## Context

At Videomagic, I led product marketing and GTM for new AI video capabilities.

The challenge was translating technical functionality into a clear customer value proposition.

## The Challenge

AI features can easily become feature-led messaging:

"Here is what the model can do."

Instead, I focused on:

**Capability → Use Case → Customer Outcome**

## GTM Approach

I worked across:

- Product positioning
- Launch messaging
- Landing pages
- Use-case narratives
- Onboarding
- Demos
- Sales enablement
- Email and lifecycle communication
- Customer education

I also worked with Product and Engineering to ensure technical capabilities and implementation considerations were represented accurately.

## Outcome

One multi-variant AI feature launch increased:

**Content output per user by 30%+**

I also developed API and SDK use-case narratives for technology and enterprise partners, including Microsoft, Zoho and Rubik.ai.

## Key Takeaway

Good product marketing doesn't simplify the product by hiding complexity.

It makes the complexity understandable — and connects it to a reason for the customer to care.
